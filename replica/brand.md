## Candidates considered

StudioFlat (apartment collision), Doorweek, Keepdoor, Openweek, Weekdoor, Takeless. ClassKeep and VisitKeep are live studio products with the same fee story — do not use.

## Palette

Ink teal `#0b3d3b` on cool mist field `#d8e4df` with frosted glass surfaces. Checked with `contrast.py`. Not taken from a reference brand.

## Logo

A square door mark in `public/favicon.svg` and `public/icons/`. Drawn for this product. Display and body are Plus Jakarta Sans (open). Glass morphism: frosted panels, soft depth, teal atmosphere — not purple AI glass. Do not borrow EasyWeek, OpenStudio, or Mindbody letterforms.

## Voice

Short sentences. Name the price. "We add $0" is allowed. "The best studio software" is not. No invented customer quotes.
